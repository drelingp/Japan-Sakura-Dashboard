💡 Hanami Tips

🕐

Arrive Early

Popular parks fill up fast. Arrive before 9 am to secure a good spot under the trees.

🍱

Pack a Bento

Bring a picnic bento with onigiri, karaage, and sakura mochi for the full experience.

🌦️

Watch the Forecast

Petals fall quickly in rain or wind. Check the 7-day forecast before planning your visit.

🚃

Use Public Transit

Parking near popular spots is nearly impossible. Take the train or a local bus.

📷

Golden Hour Magic

The soft light around sunset makes sakura photos truly stunning — don't miss it.

🏮

Night Sakura (夜桜)

Many parks light up the trees at night. Yozakura viewing is magical and less crowded.
